"WORLD IN NUMBERS" — a navy-and-cream one-page broadsheet summing up Thursday 19 November 2026 in 35 stories, all first reported that day. A giant serif date masthead, region and field count bars, a day-in-numbers strip, three illustrated lead stories, twelve illustrated small cards, a numbered-pin world map, the ALSO ON list (16–35) in three columns, and a DATE CHECK footer listing the 17–18 November stories deliberately left out. Every headline and number is exact programmatic text; the card illustrations are AI-generated flat illustrations matched to their stories.
